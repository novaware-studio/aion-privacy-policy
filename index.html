Vigil — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Vigil ("the App") is developed and published by Novaware Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Data We Collect

Data type Stored where Purpose
Alarm settings & app preferences On your device only Core app functionality
Anonymous purchase ID RevenueCat servers Manage in-app purchases & entitlements
Google account email (if Calendar sync is enabled) On your device only (OAuth token) Sync alarms to Google Calendar
Custom alarm sounds (imported files) On your device only Personalized alarm sounds

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent any data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases (Pro unlock and Tip Jar). RevenueCat processes an anonymous, randomly generated user ID and purchase transaction data from Google Play. It does not receive your name, email, or any personal information from us. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Google Sign-In & Google Calendar API

If you choose to enable the optional Google Calendar sync feature, the App requests access to your Google Calendar via OAuth 2.0. The App uses this access solely to create, update, and delete alarm events in a dedicated "Vigil Alarms" calendar. We do not read your existing calendar events, and the OAuth token is stored only on your device. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

Google Play

The App is distributed via Google Play. Google may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control data collected by Google Play itself.

4. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Why
Exact alarms Schedule alarms at precise times
Notifications Show alarm notifications and ring screen
Full-screen intent Display alarm over lock screen
Foreground service (media) Play alarm sound reliably
Vibrate Vibrate during alarm ring
Boot completed Re-schedule alarms after device restart
Internet In-app purchases and optional Calendar sync
Battery optimization exemption Ensure alarms fire in Doze mode
Sensors (accelerometer) Shake-to-dismiss wake-up mission
Storage (Android 12 and below only) Import custom alarm sounds

5. Data Storage & Security

All alarm data and app settings are stored locally on your device using encrypted application storage (Hive). No alarm data is transmitted to any server. If you use the manual backup feature (Pro), the exported file is saved to a location you choose on your device — we never receive it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete all data: Uninstall the App. All local data is removed.
  • Clear app data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Vigil → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Revoke Google Calendar access: Visit your Google Account permissions.
  • RevenueCat: Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat for transaction integrity. Contact us to request deletion of your anonymous ID.

7. Children's Privacy

Vigil is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (in this case, essentially none).
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data.
  • Data portability — export your alarms via the backup feature.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, most of these rights are satisfied by design. For any requests, contact us at hello@novawarestudio.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
